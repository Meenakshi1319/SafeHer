╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                         COMMON MISTAKES                                   ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

❌ WRONG: 9876543210          (missing country code and +)
❌ WRONG: 919876543210        (missing +)
❌ WRONG: +91 98765 43210     (has spaces)
❌ WRONG: +91-9876543210      (has dash)
❌ WRONG: (+91) 9876543210    (has parentheses)

✅ CORRECT: +919876543210     (+ sign, country code, no spaces)
